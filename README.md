Description:
I made a cookie cutter with my parents and I's nicknames! I used solidworks and a image of the writing of my name to trace off of.
Why I made this project:
I'm leaving to university soon, and I wanted something to leave my parents. I though this would be kind of cool and would give them a laugh!
